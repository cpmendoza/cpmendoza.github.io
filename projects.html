Research Projects

Hedging Targeted Risks with Reinforcement Learning (ASTIN Bulletin, submitted 2025)
Developed a reinforcement learning (RL) framework to hedge life insurance guarantees. Combined adaptive AI decision-making with Shapley explainability and deep statistical analysis. Impact: Reduced global risk exposure more effectively than traditional hedging, while avoiding unwanted risk transfers.

Deep Hedging with Implied Volatility Surface (Management Science, submitted 2025)
Built AI models that integrate entire implied volatility surfaces into hedging strategies. Used deep neural networks to capture market signals beyond price information. Impact: Produced more resilient hedges under market stress, outperforming benchmark financial models.

Is Deep Hedging vs Delta Hedging a Statistical Arbitrage? (Finance Research Letters, 2024)
Compared AI-driven hedging strategies with classical delta hedging. Applied large-scale backtesting and rigorous statistical arbitrage tests. Impact: Demonstrated consistent outperformance of AI methods, showing real profit potential.

Volatility Feedback in Deep Hedging (Mathematical Finance, submitted 2024)
Enhanced AI hedging strategies with volatility feedback information. Designed hybrid models combining machine learning and volatility-driven signals. Impact: Significantly improved hedging accuracy and risk control in volatile markets.

Parameter Estimation for Stable Continuous-State Branching Processes (Working Paper, 2025)
Developed new statistical methods for estimating parameters of complex stochastic processes. Combined Laplace inversion techniques with probabilistic modeling. Impact: Created robust tools for analyzing systemic risk and other phenomena under uncertainty.
